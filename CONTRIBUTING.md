# Contributing to Nexus Uplift

Thank you for your interest in this project.

## This Repository Is Not Open to External Contributions

The Nexus Uplift Foundation maintains full and exclusive ownership of all code, content, design, and assets in this repository. **We are not accepting pull requests, issues, feature suggestions, or any other form of external contribution at this time.**

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Bug reports or bug fixes
- Feature requests or feature implementations
- Refactoring or code cleanup
- Documentation changes
- Design or UI suggestions
- Dependency updates
- Translations

Any pull requests submitted will be closed without review.

## Why

The work in this repository is developed and maintained internally by the Foundation's team. Accepting outside contributions would require a legal framework (contributor agreements, IP assignments) that we do not currently have in place. Keeping the codebase under sole internal control is the right choice for our mission at this stage.

## If You Found a Security Issue

Security disclosures are the one exception. If you discover a genuine security vulnerability, please contact us privately before disclosing it publicly:

**Email:** hello@nexusuplift.org  
**Subject line:** `[SECURITY]`

We will respond within 5 business days.

## If You Want to Support the Foundation

We welcome support in ways other than code:

- **Volunteer** as a health educator or community organiser
- **Partner** with us as an organisation or NGO
- **Invite us** to your school or community
- **Donate** to support our work

Reach us at [nexusuplift.org/contact](https://nexusuplift.org/contact) or hello@nexusuplift.org.
